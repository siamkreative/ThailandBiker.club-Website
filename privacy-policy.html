This Privacy Policy describes how ThailandBiker.club ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

1.Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your email address and full name. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from that provider.
  • Profile information: You may optionally add a profile photo.
  • User-submitted content: When you submit a new place (motorcycle shop, rental, repair service, etc.), we collect the name, address, contact details, photos, and geographic coordinates you provide.
  • Location (when submitting places): We request access to your device's location solely to help you pin the exact coordinates of a place you are adding. We do not track your location in the background.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect information about how you interact with the app — screens visited, searches performed, places viewed, favorites added, and similar in-app actions.
  • Device information: Firebase Analytics may collect device type, operating system version, and a resettable advertising identifier.
  • Local cache: We temporarily store a list of categories on your device (expires after 7 days) to reduce load times. No personal data is stored locally beyond your cached session.

4.Data Retention

We retain your account and profile data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 6). Analytics data is retained according to the default Firebase Analytics retention schedule (up to 14 months). Local cache data on your device expires automatically after 7 days.

5.Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 20 in Thailand, where parental consent is required under the PDPA for minors under 20). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6.Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Withdraw consent for analytics tracking (via your device's advertising settings or by contacting us)
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: privacy@thailandbiker.club

We will respond within 30 days.

7.Security

We use industry-standard security practices including TLS encryption in transit and row-level security policies on our database. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8.International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside Thailand. Supabase stores data in the EU. Firebase processes data in the United States. We rely on these providers' data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses for such transfers.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending a notification through the app. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
