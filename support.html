Reporting incorrect place information

If a place listing has wrong hours, a disconnected phone number, or is permanently closed, email us at support@thailandbiker.club with the place name and what needs to be corrected. We'll update or remove it promptly.

Account issues

  • Forgot your password: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the sign-in screen to receive a reset link by email.
  • Can't sign in with Google or Facebook: Ensure the ThailandBiker.club app has permission to access your account in your device's app settings.
  • Delete your account: Email support@thailandbiker.club with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will permanently delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

Reporting inappropriate content

If you see content that violates our Terms of Use, please email support@thailandbiker.club with a description and, if possible, a screenshot.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, see our Privacy Policy or email privacy@thailandbiker.club.

Reporting a bug? Please include:

  • Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 17.4)
  • The app version (found in Settings within the app)
  • A description of what happened and what you expected

ThailandBiker.club is a community-driven project. We appreciate your feedback and patience.
