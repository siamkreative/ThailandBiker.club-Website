Account

Delete your ThailandBiker.club account

You can permanently delete your account and personal data at any time — from inside the app, or by sending us a request from this page. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Last updated:1 September 2026
This cannot be undone. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and personal data. Once complete, it cannot be restored and you will need to create a new account to use the app again.

1.Delete from inside the app (preferred)

This is the fastest method. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

  1. Open the ThailandBiker.club app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and profile data are erased right away.

2.Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email our privacy team and we'll delete your account for you. Send your request from the email address associated with your account if possible, so we can verify it.

Email a deletion request

If the button doesn't open your mail app, send an email manually to privacy@thailandbiker.club with the subject "Account deletion request" and include the email address on your account.

We action emailed requests within 30 days and will confirm by email once your account has been deleted.

3.What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile
  • Your email address
  • Your display name
  • Your profile photo
  • Your favorites

4.What is kept

Places and events you submitted stay in the app so that other riders can still rely on them, but they are anonymised — they are no longer linked to you or your identity. We retain this anonymised content for content integrity and moderation history.
