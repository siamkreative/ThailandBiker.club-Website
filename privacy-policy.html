This Privacy Policy describes how ThailandBiker.club ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

1.Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Email address: Collected when you create an account, whether you sign up with an email address or use "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Facebook" (in which case we receive your email address from that provider). We use it for authentication and account management.
  • Display name: Optional. If you set one, it is shown on your profile and next to content you submit.
  • Profile photo: Optional. Stored only if you choose to upload one.
  • Content you submit: When you submit a place or an event (motorcycle shop, rental, repair service, ride, meet, etc.), we collect the name, address, contact details, photos, geographic coordinates, and other details you provide.
  • Location (when submitting content): If you choose to, you can use your device's location to pin the exact coordinates of a place or event you are adding. Those coordinates are then part of the public listing. We do not track your location in the background.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Account identifier: An internal user ID we assign to your account to link it to your profile and submissions.
  • App activity: We use Firebase Analytics to collect information about how you interact with the app — screens viewed, places and events viewed, searches performed, favorites added, and shares.
  • Device and usage data: Firebase Analytics also collects a Firebase installation/instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, and an approximate (city-level) location derived from your IP address.
  • Local cache: We temporarily store a list of categories on your device (expires after 7 days) to reduce load times. No personal data is stored locally beyond your cached session.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or transmit your precise GPS location. The "Near Me" feature reads your device location only on your device, to sort nearby places by distance. Those coordinates are never sent to our servers or to any third party.

4.Data Retention

We retain your account and profile data (email address, display name, profile photo, favorites) for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time — see thailandbiker.club/delete-account or Section 6.

Places and events you submitted are not deleted when you close your account. Instead they are anonymised — unlinked from your identity — and retained so that other riders can still rely on them and for moderation history.

Analytics data is retained according to the default Firebase Analytics retention schedule (up to 14 months). Local cache data on your device expires automatically after 7 days.

5.Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 20 in Thailand, where parental consent is required under the PDPA for minors under 20). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6.Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data — go to thailandbiker.club/delete-account, or use Profile → Delete Account in the app
  • Withdraw consent for analytics tracking (via your device's advertising settings or by contacting us)
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: privacy@thailandbiker.club

We will respond within 30 days.

7.Security

We use industry-standard security practices including TLS encryption in transit and row-level security policies on our database. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8.International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside Thailand, including by Supabase and by Google (Firebase, Maps, and sign-in), which process data in the United States and other regions. We rely on these providers' data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses for such transfers.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending a notification through the app. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
